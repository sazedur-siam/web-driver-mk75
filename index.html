Lingbao MK75 Driver

A free, unofficial web driver for the Lingbao MK75 keyboard. It does everything the official Windows "LINGBAO Keyboard Driver" does, with nothing to install, in Chrome, Edge or Opera on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS. Connect over the USB cable, the 2.4 GHz receiver or Bluetooth.

  • Lighting: all 16 MK75 effects with brightness, speed and custom colour palettes
  • Per-key RGB editor with gradients, region selection and an eyedropper
  • Live effects: screen sync, music visualiser and custom animated effects
  • Key mapping and remapping, including the Fn layer and media keys
  • Macros, game mode, side lights and a key tester
  • Profiles, shareable presets and full backups
